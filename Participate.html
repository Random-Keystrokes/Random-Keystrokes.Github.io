Key information to consider
Keystroke Biometrics Data Collection

Voluntary Consent

You are being asked to volunteer for a research study. It is your choice whether to participate or not. There will be no penalty or loss of benefits to which you are otherwise entitled if you choose not to participate or to discontinue participation.

Purpose:

This research study collects keystroke data for human research purposes.

The purpose of this research is to explore patterns in human typing of random keystroke

It is expected that approximately 40 individuals will be participating in this research.

You are invited to participate because you are

  • Age 18 or older
  • You are English language proficient
  • You have access to a keyboard and ability to type
  • You can decide to agree to participate

Procedures and Activities:

You will be asked to complete 5 one minute session of typing letters on your computer keyboard randomly.

There is no script to follow and you do not have to type actual words.

No personal information will be stored or transmitted. The following will be recorded and transmitted to the research:

  • Key press duration (how long each key is held)
  • Flight time (time between releasing one key and pressing the next)

Duration:

Your time commitment will be approximately 20 to 25 minutes.

Risks:

Some of the foreseeable risks or discomforts of your participation. include a minimal risk of breach of confidentiality, even though the survey is anonymous and collected through Github and Binghamton University’s secure Google forms.

Alternatives: Participation in this study is voluntary and the only alternative is to not participate.

By clicking "I Consent" and participating, you agree to these terms.

Random Keystroke Collection

Please type random keystrokes in the box below for 60 seconds. Try to type naturally without any specific pattern.
