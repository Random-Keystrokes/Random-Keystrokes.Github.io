Conference Presentations

Random Keystroke Dynamics for Pattern Recognition and Authentication Modeling Presented

Jacqueline Blake · NERCCS 2026, the Ninth Northeast Regional Conference on Complex Systems · University of Rochester · Spring 2026

Presented preliminary findings on using unstructured, random keystroke timing (dwell time, flight time, and inter-key intervals) as a behavioral biometric for continuous user authentication, and outlined the data collection methodology used in this study.
